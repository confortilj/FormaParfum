How it works

A clear framework for building your scent

We break perfume into simple decisions so you never have to guess. Every custom bottle follows the same architecture: top, heart, and base, chosen from our scent families.

1. Choose your mood

Decide what you want the fragrance to say: bright, soft, dark, intimate, loud. This defines your main scent family.

2. Pick scent families

Select your top, heart, and base families (for example: Citrus • Floral • Woody). We show you how they interact.

3. Fine-tune the notes

Within each family we narrow down to specific accords like bergamot, jasmine, or cedar, guided by our blending rules.

4. We mix & bottle

You approve the formula, we blend it, name it, and send you your finished bottle plus a record of the recipe.

Custom experience

Forma Session: build your own perfume

You don’t need to “know perfume” to make something beautiful. We use a clear framework and a limited palette so you can focus on instinct instead of chemistry.

What you get

  • Guided 1:1 session in our studio or over video.
  • A finished 50 ml bottle of your custom fragrance, labeled with your chosen name.
  • Formula archived so you can reorder, tweak, or build flanker scents later.
  • Optional add-ons: travel spray, discovery vials for friends, layerable ‘sketches’.

Before you book

  • Choose in-studio (CDMX) or remote. For remote, we ship a discovery kit first.
  • Have 2–3 reference scents in mind (things you like or hate).
  • Expect to smell a lot. Comfortable clothes, clear nose, and a bit of patience recommended.

FAQ & Contact

Good to know before you order

Where are you based?

Forma Parfum works out of Mexico City. We currently ship domestically and to select international locations; if your country isn’t listed at checkout, email us and we’ll see what’s possible.

How long does a custom fragrance take?

The live session is usually 60–75 minutes. After that, we mix, macerate, and bottle your perfume. Most orders ship within 10–14 days depending on volume and season.

Can I reorder my formula later?

Yes. We store your formula under a unique ID. You can reorder the exact same blend or request minor adjustments (stronger, softer, more of a certain family).

Do you work with events, teams, or wedding scenting?

Absolutely. We create themed scent bars, group blending sessions, or custom scents for weddings and launches. Reach out with dates, size, and budget so we can propose options.
