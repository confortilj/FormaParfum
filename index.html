FAQ & Contact

Where are bottles made?

In the Hamptons studio, blended with organic alcohol and naturally derived absolutes. Each bottle is hand-poured, rested, and wrapped in linen labels.

How do I customize a bottle?

Toggle “Add bottle customization” before checkout. We’ll engrave or print your chosen name or event wording. Custom pricing uses a separate Stripe link (+$20).

Shipping & maceration time

Small batches rest for 5–7 days to settle. Domestic shipping from the Hamptons usually arrives within 3–5 business days after rest.
